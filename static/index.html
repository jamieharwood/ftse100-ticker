FTSE 100 Charts daily prices in pence

    Trading simulator backtest on daily closing prices over the selected date range

    Each day the simulator compares a stock's closing price with its moving average. It buys when the price dips below the average by the set amount, then sells once the sale would beat the full cost of the purchase (price, commission and stamp duty, plus the sell commission) by the profit target, or when the stop loss is hit. Set each stock's starting balance on its chart. This is a simulation on past prices only.
