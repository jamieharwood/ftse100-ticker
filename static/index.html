FTSE 100 Charts daily prices in pence

    Trading simulator backtest on daily closing prices over the selected date range

    Starting balances

    one per selected stock; changing the default updates any you haven't edited

    Each day the simulator compares a stock's closing price with its moving average. It buys when the price dips below the average by the set amount, then sells once the sale would beat the full cost of the purchase (price, commission and stamp duty, plus the sell commission) by the profit target, or when the stop loss is hit. Buy and sell markers and each stock's trades appear on its chart in the Stock graphs tab. This is a simulation on past prices only.
